## 3. Constructive Recommendations for Future Cohorts

To further elevate the impact of upcoming FCAJ training cohorts, I propose the following enhancements:

### 3.1. Introduce Serverless Container Orchestration (Amazon ECS on AWS Fargate)
- Following Week 6's containerization module on Docker and ECR, introducing hands-on deployment to **Amazon ECS on AWS Fargate** would expose students to modern container orchestration without the overhead of manual EC2 instance management.

### 3.2. Incorporate 1-on-1 Architecture Review Checkpoints
- During Weeks 9 and 10 when students formulate their graduation project blueprints, scheduling dedicated 20-30 minute 1-on-1 architecture review sessions with mentors would help identify security vulnerabilities, scaling bottlenecks, and hidden costs before implementation begins.

### 3.3. Integrate Real-World Chaos Engineering Scenarios
- Introducing controlled failure injection exercises (such as simulating Availability Zone outages, web tier CPU spikes, or DynamoDB throughput throttling) would empower interns to practice real-time debugging, automated Auto Scaling adjustments, and CloudWatch alert tuning.
