3. Technical Deep-Dive Sessions

SessionTechnical Core & Architectural Insights
1. Community Update
Speakers: Ho Viet Anh & Phong Pham
  • Review of AWS Community Vietnam milestones during the first half of 2026.
  • Upcoming pipeline of technical deep-dive workshops, Immersion Days, and student hackathons.
  • Programs supporting cloud adoption and certification pathways for developers and open-source contributors.
2. OpenClaw – Open-Source AI Agents
Speaker: Tuan Vu
  • Exploration of OpenClaw: The evolutionary transition from standard prompt-and-response LLMs to Autonomous AI Agents.
  • The triad architecture of autonomous agents:
    • Planning: Breaking complex problems into deterministic execution DAGs.
    • Memory: Short-term working context combined with vector-based long-term retrieval.
    • Tool Calling / Action: Direct invocation of cloud APIs and transactional backends.
  • Practical methods for optimizing inference costs and horizontal scaling across AWS containers and serverless environments.
3. From AI Trends to Business Value
Speakers: Nguyen Thu & Nam La
  • Distinguishing technological hype from measurable business outcome to avoid unvalidated AI capital expenditures.
  • High-ROI enterprise patterns:
    • Automated document extraction and business parsing workflows.
    • Enterprise knowledge retrieval with verifiable source attribution (RAG).
    • Omnichannel automated assistant workflows fortified with rigorous safety guardrails.
  • Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) models when scaling generative workloads on cloud platforms.
4. Ship Fast with AI, Not by
Speaker: Henry (Duc) Bui
  • The paradigm of "With AI, Not By AI": AI acts as an engineering accelerator for coding, test scaffolding, and refactoring.
  • Software engineers must retain absolute stewardship over system architecture, security auditing, and verification.
  • Lean shipping practices: Iterative MVP delivery, continuous validation, and tight feedback loops.
5. Tea Break & Networking
Open Networking Area
  • Direct discussions with AWS architects and community peers at the AWS Hanoi office.
  • Guidance on cloud career tracks, certification roadmaps, and internship opportunities.

5. Practical Application to the Capstone Project

The engineering insights gained from this meetup directly shaped the architecture of the Serverless Hybrid Document OCR, Parsing & Technical Translation Platform on AWS:

Platform ComponentApplied Engineering PracticeMeasured Outcome
Hybrid Routing PipelineAdopted cost-optimization guidance: Classify document characteristics to route digital text to local fast-path engines.0.31s latency on structured documents; 95% reduction in API inference costs.
Serverless Event PipelineImplemented asynchronous event-driven triggers based on S3 object ingress.S3 Event Notification dispatching AWS Lambda in 214 ms, writing directly to DynamoDB.
Configuration SecurityAdhered to the "With AI, Not By AI" principle: Strict isolation of credentials and KMS encryption.Zero credential leakage; dynamic parameter loading from AWS SSM Parameter Store.
